# Cookies

![A golden chocolate-chip cookie with a little bite missing](images/policies/cookies.png)

*Updated November 14, 2026*

A cookie is a tiny note a website saves in your browser. It helps the site
remember you between clicks.

## What we use

ttrek.sh uses an essential account cookie to keep you signed in and help protect
account forms. It holds a random ID, not your password. In production, its name
starts with `__Host-web_user_`.

We do not use advertising cookies or cookies that follow you around other sites.

## You are in control

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. The docs and package
pages still work, but signing in and account forms need the account cookie.

Signing out ends your signed-in session. The cookie is a browser-session cookie;
some browsers keep these when restoring tabs.

Read our [Privacy Policy](/docs/policies/privacy-policy) for more about data.
