# Privacy Policy

![A blue shield with a cream padlock protecting personal information](images/policies/privacy-policy.png)

*Updated November 14, 2026*

JERILY LTD runs ttrek.sh. This page explains what information the service uses
and why.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, email, protected password, and sign-in and
  security records when you create an account.
- **Visit details:** technical information such as your IP address, browser,
  requested pages, and errors, to run and protect the site.
- **Package-use details:** the ttrek tool can send an environment ID, system
  details, package names and versions, and whether an installation worked.

## Why we use it

We use account details to provide your account. We use technical records to
keep the service working, stop abuse, and understand package use. In legal terms,
these purposes are providing the service you request and our legitimate interests
in running a reliable service.

Hosting and email providers, including AWS, process data to help us run ttrek.sh.
Package statistics and service-health summaries may be public.

## Your choices

You can edit your profile in [Your account](/user). Set `TTREK_TELEMETRY=0` before
running ttrek to turn off its optional telemetry; normal server request logs can
still be recorded. See [Cookies](/docs/policies/cookies) for browser controls.

You can ask about stored data, how long it is kept, or request access,
correction, deletion, or a copy. You can also
object to certain uses. Some records may need to stay for security or legal reasons.
You may complain to your local
[data protection authority](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en).
